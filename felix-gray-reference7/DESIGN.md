# Creative Direction & Technical Specification — `videobrut 7.mp4` Edit (Inspired by `reference 7.mp4`)

## 1. Reference Analysis (`reference 7.mp4`)
- **Format & Specs**: `720x1280` (9:16 vertical), `25.00 fps`, `28.20s` duration (upscaled and mastered at `1080x1920` Full HD for our deliverable).
- **Color Palette**:
  - **Studio Midnight Void**: `#020512` to `#050C24`
  - **Electric Cobalt Glow**: `#1E50FF` / `#2558FF` (radial light blooms, 3D circular orbs, brush-script accents, and partial letter-glows)
  - **Crimson Alert Vignette**: `#090103` center to `#4E0512` outer radial vignette with `#FF2442` glowing script
  - **Glassmorphic Laptop UI Surface**: `#141720` card fill, `#2B3245` 1px specular border, `#8E96AA` secondary metadata, `#FFFFFF` primary headlines
- **Typography Hierarchy**:
  1. **3-Tier & 4-Tier Asymmetric Chest Lockups**:
     - Line 1 (Setup): `Montserrat Bold` (`~42px` at 1080w), `#FFFFFF`
     - Line 2 (Core Heavy Noun/Verb): `Montserrat Black` (`~82–92px` at 1080w), `#FFFFFF` or `#C8D0DF`
     - Line 3 (Signature Brush Accent): `Mr Dafoe Regular` (`~102–116px` at 1080w), `#2558FF` (or `#FF2442` in crimson scenes) with an outer neon bloom overlapping the baseline of Line 2 by `16px`.
  2. **Single-Word Kinetic Chest Captions with Partial Inner Blue Glow**:
     - Centered at `(x=540, y=1110)` in `Montserrat ExtraBold` (`72px`).
     - Key stressed words feature a localized radial electric-blue light bloom (`#2558FF`) across the leading or central letters (`in` of `inquiry`, `in` of `inconsistent`), tinting those characters icy cyan-blue while the remaining characters stay crisp white.
  3. **Dual 3D Glowing Blue Circular Chest Badges**:
     - Two 3D glassmorphic cobalt-blue circular orbs (`radius=105px`, navy-to-cobalt radial gradient fill, bright `#4882FF` specular rim, outer blue bloom, and crisp white vector iconography) popping in with spring physics below the single-word chest captions (`t=18.25–22.00s` in `reference 7.mp4`: Megaphone + Handshake).
  4. **Full-Screen B-Roll Cutaway #1 — 3D Dark Laptop UI (`t=4.50–7.30s` in `reference 7.mp4`)**:
     - A sleek 3D dark-matte laptop on a midnight-blue studio backdrop (`#020512` + royal-blue radial backlight) slowly pushes in and pans as glassmorphic notification rows stack vertically one by one with avatars, timestamps, and live-typed subtext alongside a right-hand detail pane.
  5. **Full-Screen B-Roll Cutaway #2 — 3D Blue Circle Icon Shatter -> Crimson Red Vignette + Stacked Red Script (`t=12.00–15.50s` in `reference 7.mp4`)**:
     - A large 3D cobalt-blue circular handshake badge scales in on a midnight-blue canvas, cracks, and **shatters explosively into ~120 3D faceted white & cobalt polygonal shards**, immediately cross-fading to a **dark crimson-red radial vignette** where a 4-line stacked lockup (`Montserrat Bold` + `Montserrat Black` + crimson `Mr Dafoe` script) reveals line by line.
  6. **Final Signature CTA Lockup (`t=26.00–28.20s` in `reference 7.mp4`)**:
     - Centered 3-line stack: White `Mr Dafoe` script (*`Comment`*) + Electric-Blue `Montserrat Black Italic` (*`'Rhythm'`*) + White `Montserrat Bold` (`or DM me`).
